Account and Data Deletion Guide

This page provides a clear guide on how users can request the deletion of their account and related data.

1. App and Developer Information

App Name: QS Remit

Developer Name:NNP Korea.

Contact Email: developers@qsremit.net

2. How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account deletion by sending an email to:
developers@qsremit.net

3. Types of Data Deleted or Retained

Immediately Deleted: Login information, basic profile

Retained Temporarily: Financial transaction data (up to 7 years in accordance with legal requirements)

Exceptions: Data that must be retained by law will be stored for the legally mandated period.

4. Additional Information

Once your account is deleted, you will no longer be able to use our services with that account, and all related data will be deleted or destroyed after a certain retention period.
